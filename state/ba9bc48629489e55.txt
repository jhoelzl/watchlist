Lindy Cats Specials
Lindy Cats Intensive Days with Ana and Alberto
15. - 19.10.2026
Four days full of swing dancing and authentic solo jazz with Ana and Alberto. Look forward to focused training, new impulses for technique and musicality as well as lots of time for dancing, experimentation and development.
On the weekend we're continuing intensively at our Community Exchange.
Tracks
Open Level - Fun Track (25 €)
You can do triple steps, change of place und send out without any difficulties.
Intermediates & Explorers (25 €)
You can do triple steps, change of place, send out und swing out without any difficulties.
Advanced (50 €)
You regularly attend socials. You can do swing out, circle, tuck turns, sugar pushes, charleston basic und hand to hand without any difficulties.
Teachers (50 €)
You are teaching Lindy Hop (for the Lindy Cats).

Registration is closed.

BRG Kepler, Keplerstraße 1, 8020 Graz (Mon)
Swing Dance & Community Exchange with the Swing Freaks from Triest
16. - 18.10.2026
Two swing communities, one dance floor: an occasion to share laughs, moves, and good times!
After the wonderful weekend in Trieste in spring, it is now time to welcome our friends in Graz.
