Ivy Live Translation
Chaque conversation est une occasion
Every Conversation is a Match
Key Features
Real-time bidirectional
translation of conversations
In today’s globally connected world, clear and instant communication is essential – especially in high-traffic environments like airports, public transport hubs, and logistic checkpoints. That’s why Commend’s live translation solution is transforming the way intercom systems serve diverse populations.
By integrating real-time translation into existing Commend intercom infrastructure, we eliminate language barriers at help points, information counters, and access control stations. Whether assisting travelers at an airport, coordinating transport logistics, or guiding passengers in public transit, our solution ensures that every message is understood – instantly and accurately.
Ivy Live Translation for Logistics
This not only improves the experience for non-native speakers but also
empowers control room operators to respond more efficiently and with greater confidence.
The result: smoother operations, faster response times, and a truly inclusive communication experience, wherever Commend is in place.

Ivy Dashboard
The secret behind Ivy
The key to Ivy’s talents lies in its ability to learn (by training) about real-life topics or scenarios and the proper conversational patterns in dealing with them.
Ivy Virtual Assistant comes with a suite of powerful tools to monitor relevant performance metrics:
Statistics Dashboard
The Ivy Dashboard monitors crucial conversation quality and performance indicators, including reaction times, calls per day, call duration, and per-call iterations. It provides visual overviews like the KPI Dashboard, Call Timeline, and Call Heatmap. Additionally, it helps in training and optimizing Ivy's behavior as needed.
Conversation Analytics
The Ivy Dashboard provides drill-down history to understand Ivy's responses. It stores complete conversation transcripts for reference and analysis, documents situational analytics, and offers audio recordings if required. It also complies with local data protection regulations, ensuring data safety and security.
